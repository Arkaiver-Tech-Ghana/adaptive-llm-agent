# Grand Vista Hotel Policies

## Cancellation
Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before
check-in. Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in are charged one
night's rate. No-shows are charged the full stay.

## Payment
A valid card is required to hold a booking. Full payment is due at
check-in; we accept all major credit cards and cash.

## Minimum age to book
Guests must be at least 18 years old to book a room and check in.

## Smoking
Grand Vista Hotel is entirely non-smoking, including balconies and
outdoor common areas. A cleaning fee applies if this policy isn't
followed.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours run from 10:00pm to 7:00am out of consideration for other
guests.
