# Grand Vista Hotel FAQ

**What time is check-in and check-out?**
Check-in is from 3:00pm, check-out is by 11:00am. Early check-in and late
check-out can be requested at the front desk and are granted based on
availability.

**Is breakfast included?**
Breakfast is included for Suite bookings. Standard and Deluxe guests can
add breakfast for $15 per person per day.

**Do you have parking?**
Yes, free self-parking is available for all hotel guests in the on-site
lot.

**Is WiFi included?**
Yes, free WiFi is included in every room and throughout the hotel's public
areas.

**Do you allow pets?**
Small pets under 25lbs are welcome in Standard and Deluxe rooms for a
one-time $40 pet fee. Suites are pet-free.

**Is the hotel accessible?**
Yes, we have accessible rooms and step-free access to all public areas —
ask at booking if you need an accessible room and we'll do our best to
accommodate you.
