# KampusCrave FAQ

**Do you take campus meal plan cards?**
Yes, both carts accept the standard campus meal plan card alongside cash
and card payments.

**Is there a vegetarian option?**
Yes — the Veggie Burger, and all sides and drinks are vegetarian.

**Do you deliver?**
Not currently. Both locations are pickup-only carts.

**Can I customize a burger?**
Yes, ask the cart staff to add or remove toppings at no extra charge for
substitutions within the same burger.
